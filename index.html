Unionville Bingo Rules

How to Play:

  • Stroll down historic Main Street Unionville and look around for the items on your card.
  • Tap any item when you spot it to stamp it as found.
  • Tap again to unmark if tapped by mistake.
  • Complete any 5 in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to score a BINGO!
  • Your board and progress are automatically saved, so you can lock your phone or close the browser anytime.
